**Reading this.** Camera sites (and the technicians at them) are what a district mostly has to provide; every other stage is lightly used. With JPEG photographs even 2G-grade links carry a day's uploads (store and forward overnight). One GPU server grades a district's patients in a few percent of its time. Specialist time grows with the share of patients flagged, so specificity matters: an uncalibrated camera like IDRiD more than doubles the review load, and review without the 30-second annotated report (120 s per case) nearly quadruples it. The stress rows show when reviewers and bandwidth start to need more than the minimum.
