# Optic disc and fovea localisation (IDRiD localisation test set)

Generated by `stage2_structure/dl/validateLocalisation.m`. 103 photographs (4288x2848) with expert centre labels. The disc is the largest confident region of the lesion network's optic-disc output; the fovea is estimated 2.5 disc diameters temporal to the disc (`estimateFovea.m`), which the report uses to centre the quadrants and to find the macula. DD = disc diameter, taken per photograph as the expert disc-to-fovea distance / 2.5.

| | Median error | Within 0.5 DD | Within 1 DD | Photographs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Optic disc centre | 39 px (0.08 DD) | 98% | 98% | 103 with a disc found (100%) |
| Fovea, estimated from the disc | 242 px (0.48 DD) | 51% | 90% | 103 |
| Fovea baseline: centre of the retina | 364 px (0.74 DD) | 35% | 83% | 103 |

The fovea estimate is used only to split the retina into quadrants and to say whether possible hard exudates lie near the macula; both are
labelled as estimates in the report. The disc channel was trained on IDRiD's segmentation photographs, a different subset from these.
