# Stage 2 vessels on DRIVE

Generated by `validation/vesselDriveEval.m`. 20 DRIVE photographs with manual vessel maps (the training set of the Kaggle mirror; the classical method is unsupervised and was not tuned on DRIVE). Mean over photographs, inside DRIVE's field-of-view masks.

| Method | Accuracy | Sensitivity | Specificity | Dice | AUC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Soares et al. 2006 (supervised; test set) | 0.9461 | 0.7332 | 0.9782 | - | 0.9614 |

Published rows: Table 4 of Memari et al., "Supervised retinal vessel segmentation from color fundus images based on matched filtering and AdaBoost classifier", PLOS ONE 2017 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5724901/). They are on DRIVE's test photographs; this mirror has no test annotations, so the comparison is indicative.

U-Net row: `stage2_structure/dl/trainVesselSegmenter.m`. Each photo is scored by a network trained on the other 3 folds, at that fold's threshold (best Dice on its own training photos). The deployed network (`Stage2_VesselUNet.mat`, applied with `segmentVesselsDL.m`) is trained on all 20 with threshold 0.49. Per photo: `vessels_drive_unet_cv.csv`.
