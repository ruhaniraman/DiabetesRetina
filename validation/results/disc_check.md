# Stage 1: is the optic disc in the picture?

Generated by `validation/disc_check.py` from the shipped gate (`backend/quality.py`, `disc_score`). It is a **warning**, never a rejection.

## 1. Does the detector find the disc? (IDRiD, 516 photographs, disc centres marked by experts)

Distance between the detected blob and the marked disc centre, as a share of the retina width: within 0.05 for **95.2%**, within 0.08 for 98.3%; median 0.033. (The disc radius is about 0.035.)

## 2. Does its confidence separate frames with the disc from frames without? (frames cut from IDRiD photographs)

523 frames with the disc fully inside, 360 with it clearly outside. AUC of the score: **0.890**.

| Group | n | 5th percentile | median | 95th percentile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole IDRiD photographs | 516 | 3.64 | 5.11 | 6.42 |
| Whole APTOS photographs | 3662 | 3.64 | 5.65 | 7.96 |
| Frames with the disc | 523 | 3.13 | 4.68 | 6.43 |
| Frames without the disc | 360 | 1.44 | 2.26 | 5.37 |

## 4. Who gets the warning? (APTOS, threshold 3.0, by disease grade)

Looking at the lowest-scoring photographs, most do show a disc: it is pale or at the edge of a macula-centred photograph, or hidden by haze or fibrous tissue in advanced disease. So some warnings are false alarms, and they are a little more common in advanced disease.

| Grade | Photos | Warned |
|---|---|---|
| No_DR | 1805 | 23 (1.3%) |
| Mild | 370 | 4 (1.1%) |
| Moderate | 999 | 13 (1.3%) |

| Proliferate_DR | 295 | 7 (2.4%) |

## Limits

- The detector is the brightest disc-sized blob. Bright lesions, glare or a bright rim can win instead, and a picture with no disc still has a brightest spot, so only a **low** score is informative. It does not prove the disc is present, and it does not check the macula.
- Ground truth exists for IDRiD only. APTOS has no disc annotations, so for APTOS only the warning rate is known, not the accuracy.
- The 'frames' are windows cut from complete photographs, not real photographs taken off-centre; real off-centre photographs also show a different rim and illumination.
- A warning is advice to check the framing. The photograph is still graded.
