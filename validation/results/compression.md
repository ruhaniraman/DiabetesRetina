# Low-bandwidth mode: does compressing the photo change the referral?

Generated by `validation/compression_eval.py`. Each held-out test photograph was graded twice by the deployed Stage 3 pipeline: as the original file, and as the app's low-bandwidth upload (longest side at most 1800 px, JPEG quality 85, as `frontend/src/utils/compressImage.js` makes it). Referral threshold 0.0964. 2G-grade link: 0.1 Mbps with 60% usable and 10% protocol overhead (Stage 5 assumptions).

APTOS originals are the competition's full-resolution PNG files (lossless, so their size overstates a camera's JPEG); IDRiD originals are the camera's own JPEG files. A decision that changes is a photo whose referral score sits close to the threshold.
